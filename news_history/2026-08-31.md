### The Week That Was: A Geopolitical Soap Opera & Tech Tidal Wave! 🎭

**🌍 Geopolitics & Conflicts: Hormuz High Stakes, Ukraine's Ordeal, and Nature's Fury!**

The **Strait of Hormuz** has been a dramatic focal point. After weeks of tension, U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM) announced Thursday evening that **all Iranian sea mines have been cleared** from international shipping lanes, declaring the Strait "open" for free transit. CENTCOM also stated its naval blockade on Iran has been "highly effective," preventing Iranian oil exports since mid-July and redirecting 75 vessels. Despite this, an oil tanker was reportedly struck by an "unidentified projectile" near Oman on Thursday, catching fire, underscoring lingering risks. Iran is also reportedly drawing up its own list of conditions for reopening the Strait, with mediators pushing Tehran to set its terms. Fresh U.S. sanctions targeting Iran, dubbed an "economic D-Day," were also announced earlier in the week.

In the **Black Sea conflict**, Russia intensified its large-scale strikes on Ukrainian cities and infrastructure throughout the week, while Ukraine continued its drone attacks on Russian refineries and e-commerce facilities. Tragically, a Friday night drone strike on an ammunition warehouse near Kyiv killed at least 38 people. Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk warned that Russia's war is escalating and threatening to engulf Europe, noting an increase in Russian-linked hybrid attacks across the continent. The UK and Ukraine also solidified a partnership to jointly develop AI tools for defense.

Nature's fury continued elsewhere. The devastating **Colombia earthquake** on August 10th saw its death toll climb to at least 331 fatalities, with 240 people still missing and extensive destruction across 16 departments. Colombia was also rattled by another magnitude 5.3 earthquake on Wednesday. A catastrophic **glacial collapse in the Nepal-Tibet** border region triggered massive flooding on August 26, killing at least 162 in Nepal and 3 in Tibet, with hundreds more missing, including foreigners. Satellite imagery confirmed that the bedrock beneath the glacier collapsed, sending a torrent of rock, ice, mud, and debris through river systems. The death toll has since risen to 750 across Nepal and China, with over 3,000 still missing.

**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Jackson Hole Jitters & Hawkish Vibes!**

Bond markets have been on a wild ride, heavily influenced by Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh's highly anticipated keynote address at the Jackson Hole symposium. Warsh's speech on Friday indicated that inflation in the U.S. is "still too high" and hinted that the central bank "may have to act," suggesting potential further rate hikes. He stressed that the Fed's 2% price stability objective is a "firm fixed target" and that recent inflation data don't show "meaningful improvement." This hawkish tone sent U.S. long-dated Treasury yields stubbornly high ahead of and during his remarks, with the 30-year near 5.173%. The U.S. dollar also strengthened, sitting near a one-week high. Warsh also criticized "forward guidance," preferring a more flexible, data-dependent approach.

**Eurozone government bond yields** remained near 15-year highs, with Germany's 10-year yield hovering around 3.25%. Markets are still heavily pricing in an **ECB rate hike in September**.

**🛢️ Oil & Energy: Hormuz Hustle & Russian Scramble!**

**Oil prices** saw a rollercoaster this week. They initially climbed due to the prolonged Hormuz standoff and escalating Middle East tensions, with Brent hovering above $90 a barrel. However, the announcement of new, aggressive U.S. sanctions against Iran led to a counterintuitive dip in prices earlier in the week, a classic "sell the news" reaction. Brent crude futures are now trading around $88.27 a barrel, while West Texas Intermediate futures are around $79.85 a barrel, poised for weekly losses, despite the latest strikes. While the secret U.S. "Roaring Lion" shipping corridor is moving oil, and mines have been cleared, the overall flow through Hormuz remains severely impacted, with total Gulf exports recently estimated at 15-16 million barrels per day, still 7-8 million bpd below pre-war levels. Meanwhile, **Russia's oil industry** is facing a desperate scramble for gasoline imports due to relentless Ukrainian drone attacks on its refineries.

**🌾 Agriculture: Crop Concerns & Black Sea Blockages!**

The agricultural world is keenly watching **U.S. crop yields**. The USDA's August WASDE report had already cut average U.S. corn (180.7 bpa) and soybean (52.7 bpa) yields due to extreme heat and dryness in the Midwest, despite increased planted acreage. The Pro Farmer Crop Tour is reportedly finding further yield disappointment, reinforcing concerns about tight U.S. supply. This has driven corn futures higher. **Wheat, corn, and soybeans** saw a robust rally on Wednesday, propelled by escalating Black Sea tensions and renewed concerns over tight U.S. supply, with wheat even going limit-up. The persistent **Black Sea conflict** continues to disrupt Ukrainian grain exports, and Russia's August wheat exports are projected to be their lowest in 16 years due to port closures, pushing global wheat prices higher. China remains an active buyer of U.S. soybeans.

**💰 Metals: Golden Glow & Industrial Strength! ✨**

**Gold and silver** surged significantly earlier in the week, hitting multi-month highs, driven by safe-haven demand amidst geopolitical uncertainty and concerns about fiscal sustainability. Gold pushed above $4,600, while silver briefly touched $70 per ounce. This rally was partly fueled by the U.S. Treasury's unexpected move to double its long-term bond buyback program. However, gold eased slightly on Wednesday and then saw declines on Monday, hitting a nearly two-week low, after Fed Chair Warsh's hawkish comments firmed up expectations of further rate hikes. Silver and platinum also slipped. Long-term, silver still faces a sixth straight annual supply deficit in 2026.

### Broader Implications: The Volatility Treadmill Continues 🎲

This past week has hammered home that global markets are on a perpetual volatility treadmill, where geopolitical shifts, monetary policy, and technological leaps are inextricably linked. The **Strait of Hormuz** remains a critical flashpoint, even with mines cleared and U.S. control asserted; the reported oil tanker strike and Iran's demands for conditions highlight ongoing risks that continue to add layers of complexity to energy markets. The **U.S. Treasury's intervention in the bond market** offered only a brief calm, as deep-seated concerns about inflation and colossal government debt continue to keep bond and FX markets on edge, putting immense pressure on both the Federal Reserve (with Warsh's hawkish hints at Jackson Hole) and the ECB. Simultaneously, the **AI revolution** is consuming vast resources, leading to colossal infrastructure investments, fierce chip competition, and significant price hikes for essential hardware. This technological arms race, with its immense demands and rising costs, is proving to be a new inflationary force, further complicating central bank decisions and adding to existing pressures from persistent global conflicts and supply chain disruptions. Vigilance and agility remain the name of the game for market participants.
