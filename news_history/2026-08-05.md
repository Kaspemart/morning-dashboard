### The Week That Was: A Geopolitical Tightrope & Tech Tangle! 🎭

The Fed's commitment to its 2% inflation target remains unwavering, with Fed Chair Kevin Warsh signaling a focus on "big questions" at his upcoming Jackson Hole speech. Across the pond, the **European Central Bank (ECB)** also delivered a "hawkish pause" last week, ready for a potential September hike if surging energy prices fuel inflation. Eurozone growth in Q2 moderated, while German inflation accelerated to a three-month high in July. The **US Dollar's** performance was mixed, often acting as a safe haven. The US Treasury also announced borrowing estimates for the July-September and October-December 2026 quarters, with expected borrowing of $739 billion and $628 billion respectively.

This technological arms race, with its immense infrastructure demands, is also adding new inflationary pressures, further complicating central bank decisions. The global market remains a high-stakes arena where geopolitical stability, monetary policy, and transformative technology are deeply intertwined, creating both volatile risks and fleeting opportunities.
