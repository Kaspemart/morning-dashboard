### The Week That Was: A Geopolitical Soap Opera & Tech Tidal Wave! 🎭

**🌍 Geopolitics: From Fragile Truce to Full-Blown Flare-Up!**
The brief respite in the US-Iran conflict ended abruptly. After signing an interim peace deal on June 15, Iran attacked three commercial vessels in the **Strait of Hormuz** on July 7-8, prompting retaliatory US airstrikes. President Trump declared the interim agreement "over," while Iran condemned US actions and claimed to have targeted US bases in Kuwait and Bahrain. The Strait, vital for 20% of global seaborne oil, has seen traffic disruptions, and maritime authorities raised its threat level to "severe." Despite the military escalation, the US affirmed that technical nuclear talks with Iran are ongoing. Elsewhere, the **Venezuela earthquake disaster** continued to worsen, with the death toll now at 3,889. Over 16,740 people were injured, and nearly 18,000 displaced, leading to urgent warnings from health agencies about disease risks.

**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Hawkish Fed & Yield Swings!**
Bond markets were on a rollercoaster this week, heavily influenced by the escalating Middle East tensions and central bank signals. The **Federal Reserve** made a significant shift, with inflation reaching 4.2% (CPI) and 4.1% (PCE), the highest since 2023. The June FOMC minutes revealed deep concerns over persistent price pressures, and Chairman Kevin Warsh indicated a strong possibility of one to two more rate hikes by year-end, completely reversing earlier market expectations of cuts. Futures markets now show a high probability of the Fed holding steady in July but a greater chance of a hike in September. US 10-year Treasury yields fluctuated, reaching around 4.49-4.56% by mid-week, driven by inflation concerns and the hawkish Fed. The US dollar significantly strengthened amidst this volatility and the renewed geopolitical risk.

**🛢️ Oil & Energy: Strait Back in the Spotlight!**
Oil markets experienced whiplash as the US-Iran ceasefire crumbled. After an initial slide below $71 a barrel of Brent early in the week on hopes of Strait of Hormuz normalization, prices **surged dramatically**. By July 8, Brent crude jumped nearly 6% to over $80 a barrel following tanker attacks in the Strait and Trump's declaration that the peace deal was "over." US retaliatory strikes pushed Brent above $79 and WTI near $74, with concerns growing over the security of oil shipments through the vital waterway. The US also revoked a waiver that had allowed Iranian oil sales.

**🌾 Agriculture: Trade Hopes & Weather Watch!**
The week saw some positive movement for agriculture. Early on (July 2), **wheat, soybeans, and corn** saw gains, fueled by renewed speculation of China buying US soybeans. US soybean stocks were reported higher than anticipated, and planted acres for soybeans also increased. Corn growers were closely monitoring the impact of heat domes on pollination and chances of rain in key regions. By July 6, grain futures, particularly soybeans, rallied sharply due to optimism about easing US-China trade barriers, pulling corn and wheat higher with them.

**💰 Metals: Geopolitics & Fed Drive Swings! ✨**
Precious metals saw considerable swings this week. Gold, silver, and platinum initially rallied on July 2 as weaker-than-expected US jobs data eased fears of aggressive Fed rate hikes, causing the dollar to weaken. However, this trend reversed mid-week, with precious metals experiencing a downturn by July 8 due to mounting geopolitical uncertainties in the Middle East and the Fed's hawkish shift. While gold, silver, and platinum futures saw increases on July 9, by July 10, international gold and silver prices registered a slight decline as the US-Iran ceasefire collapsed and the US dollar strengthened. In industrial metals, China continues to leverage export controls on rare earth materials, pushing its domestic manufacturers to compete more aggressively in global markets.
