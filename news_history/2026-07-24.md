### The Week That Was: A Geopolitical Soap Opera & Tech Tidal Wave! 🎭

**🌍 Geopolitics & Conflicts: The Strait of Flames Ignites (and Spreads)!**
The US-Iran "interim peace deal" is officially a distant memory. The US launched over a dozen **consecutive nights of airstrikes** across Iran, targeting military operations, maritime capabilities, and command centers. President Trump **reinstated a naval blockade on Iranian ports** and is now threatening direct strikes on Iranian infrastructure if ships are targeted, even hinting at hitting bridges or power plants. The **Strait of Hormuz** remains a global chokehold, with commercial shipping at severely reduced levels—down 90-95% from pre-conflict times—as Iran's IRGC claims control and warns all vessels. The conflict dangerously expanded this week, with Iran-backed **Houthi forces in Yemen attacking Saudi oil tankers in the Red Sea** and announcing a naval blockade of Saudi Arabia, causing some tankers to make U-turns. The UN and the IEA are expressing serious concerns about global energy security. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio warned ASEAN leaders that Iranian control of Hormuz would set a dangerous global precedent. The Pentagon estimates the war has cost the US $37.5 billion so far. On a separate note, the **Venezuela earthquake disaster** continues, with fatalities officially at 4,333. Ukrainian attacks on Russian vessels in the **Sea of Azov** continue to disrupt commercial shipping there. And the US is looking to restart diplomacy to end the Ukraine war, with Secretary of State Rubio meeting his Russian counterpart.

**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Tightrope Walks and Hawkish Pauses!**
The **Federal Reserve** has been on a wild ride of expectations. May's higher CPI (4.2%) and PCE (4.1%) initially suggested more rate hikes, but then came cooler June inflation data (CPI eased to 3.5%, core CPI to 2.6%, and PPI actually *fell* 0.3%). This caused traders to largely unwind bets on a July Fed hike, though a September hike remains a strong possibility (around 55%). Fed Chair Kevin Warsh acknowledged that massive AI investments might drive up short-term prices but isn't convinced it means persistent inflation. US 10-year Treasury yields hovered around 4.63% this week before rising to 4.65% today, reflecting geopolitical drama and evolving Fed expectations. The **US Dollar Index (DXY)** held firm, bolstered by Mideast risks. Across the pond, the **European Central Bank (ECB)** held rates steady today but is signaling a "hawkish pause," ready for a potential September hike if energy price surges fuel inflation. Eurozone bond yields also saw a bump due to the Middle East conflict.

**🛢️ Oil & Energy: Hormuz Hijinks Fuel Supply Fears!**
Oil prices have been a direct reflection of the escalating Strait of Hormuz conflict. After a brief dip on fleeting peace hopes, prices exploded, with **Brent crude reaching two-month highs** and WTI following suit. The US naval blockade and intensifying conflict in the Strait, compounded by Houthi attacks in the Red Sea, are fueling massive supply concerns. Goldman Sachs even warned Brent crude could top $120 if disruptions persist. The IEA head is sounding the alarm on global energy security. The seizure of a Liberian-flagged tanker today significantly amplifies these concerns.

**🌾 Agriculture: War, Weather, and Research Woes!**
The **USDA's July WASDE report** brought mixed signals: new-crop US corn ending stocks were cut due to increased export demand, soybean ending stocks held steady (with a projected record US production), and **US wheat ending stocks were lowered**, pointing to the smallest US wheat production since 1970. China continued its appetite for US soybeans. The EU adopted a Delegated Act to exclude soybean seed from deforestation regulations. Hot weather in the US Midwest remains a concern for corn and soybeans. Meanwhile, the escalating **Black Sea conflict** has severely impacted Ukrainian grain exports, keeping wheat prices elevated. Concerns are growing over the **decline in public investment in US agricultural research** since 2002, contrasting with China's booming investment in this sector.

**💰 Metals: Navigating the Headwinds! ✨**
Precious metals saw a volatile week. Gold and silver initially gained on hopes of easing Fed rate hikes but then experienced declines, caught between rising oil prices (geopolitical risk) and the Fed's ongoing hawkish stance (despite softer CPI/PPI). Platinum and palladium, driven more by industrial demand, showed mixed movements. Central banks, however, are reportedly still keen buyers on price dips. China maintains its tight grip on rare earth export controls. **Guardian Metal's Pilot Mountain Tungsten Project** in Nevada, backed by US Defense Production Act investment, released positive Pre-Feasibility Study results, signaling a potential new US tungsten source.
