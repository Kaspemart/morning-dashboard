Central banks are truly walking a tightrope! The **US Dollar** has generally held firm, acting as a safe haven amidst the geopolitical turmoil.

Oil prices have been a direct reflection of the Middle East's instability. **🌾 Agriculture: Global Headwinds & Tech Leaps!**
The **USDA's July WASDE report** (July 10) presented mixed signals: new-crop US corn ending stocks were cut due to increased export demand, soybean ending stocks held steady (with projected record US production), and US wheat ending stocks were lowered, pointing to the smallest US wheat production since 1970. Hot weather in the US Midwest remains a concern for corn and soybeans, though Iowa crops are currently rated highly. The escalating **Black Sea conflict** continues to severely impact Ukrainian grain exports, keeping wheat prices elevated. Concerns are growing over the decline in public investment in US agricultural research, contrasting with China's booming investment. New tech is emerging though: John Deere's acquisition of drone technology company Sentera aims to transform crop management, and 360 Yield is seeing success with its autonomous irrigation system, 360 Rain. Also, "right to repair" settlements are being applauded by farmers, easing the burden of equipment maintenance.
