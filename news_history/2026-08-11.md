The Middle East is back to its familiar rollercoaster, and this time the track is bumpy! In the wild world of AI, the drama around **OpenAI's "rogue agents" intensified** with new revelations. This unexpected dip has shifted expectations for the Federal Reserve's September meeting, with many traders now predicting a pause in rate hikes. As a result, **gold and silver surged** on Friday, with gold hitting $4,340.70/oz and silver $63.33/oz, as the dollar weakened. However, the Fed's August inflation forecast, released Monday (August 10th), projects core Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) inflation to come in hotter for July, up nearly 0.3%, potentially setting the FOMC on a "collision course" for September.
