### The Week That Was: A Geopolitical Soap Opera & Tech Tidal Wave! 🎭

**🌍 Geopolitics & Conflicts: Strait Saga, Red Sea Rage, and Ukrainian Resilience!**

The **Strait of Hormuz** remained a geopolitical pressure cooker all week. The U.S.-Iran ceasefire expired on Monday (August 17th) without resolution, with Iran defiantly stating the waterway will stay closed until its demands for lifted sanctions and compensation are met. Despite this, the *secret U.S. "Roaring Lion" shipping corridor* continues to move millions of barrels of oil daily through a southern channel, aiming to mitigate supply disruptions, and U.S. officials claim control and have intercepted Iranian drone and missile attacks. Still, overall shipping traffic through Hormuz remains severely suppressed, reportedly 90-95% below pre-war levels. Iran also issued new warnings that any support for fresh U.S. economic measures would be deemed an "act of war".

In the **Red Sea**, Iran-backed Houthi forces claimed another missile attack, this time on a Saudi oil tanker off Yanbu. Such attacks have led to a reported 40% drop in tanker transits through the strategic Bab el-Mandeb Strait.

The **Black Sea conflict** saw intensified strikes. Russia launched massive attacks on Ukrainian cities and infrastructure. Ukraine, in turn, escalated its drone attacks on Russian refineries and e-commerce facilities, forcing Russia to scramble for fuel imports from countries like India, Turkey, and Belarus. The UK and Ukraine also inked a partnership to jointly develop AI tools for defense and security, giving British researchers access to Ukraine's battlefield data.

The tragic toll from the **Colombia earthquake** on August 10th continues to climb, with official reports confirming at least 331 fatalities and 240 people still missing. The government declared a State of Emergency, and widespread destruction persists across 15 departments. Nuns in Colombia have even resorted to social media to plead for help for their devastated communities. Elsewhere, a devastating **glacial collapse in the Nepal-Tibet** border region triggered catastrophic flooding, leaving over 162 dead and hundreds missing.

**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Yields on a Rollercoaster!**

Bond markets were on a wild ride this week! **U.S. Treasury yields** initially soared to multi-year highs (the 30-year briefly hitting a 19-year peak above 5.30%) due to persistent inflation fears and heavy government borrowing. The U.S. Treasury then made a surprise move on Wednesday, announcing it would double its liquidity support buyback operations for longer-dated bonds, which caused yields to tumble temporarily. However, this relief was short-lived, with yields climbing again by week's end as underlying fiscal concerns persisted and the market braced for Fed Chair Warsh's speech. **Eurozone government bond yields** mirrored this tension, remaining near 15-year highs, with German officials openly blaming "Trump's war in Iran" for the surge. Markets are still heavily pricing in an **ECB rate hike in September**. Minutes from the Federal Reserve's July meeting revealed that "several" officials were prepared to raise rates, keeping a hawkish posture alive. The **U.S. Dollar** had a volatile week, reacting to economic data and geopolitical shifts.

The agricultural world is keenly watching **U.S. crop yields**. This past week has reinforced that global markets are on a perpetual volatility treadmill, where geopolitical shifts, monetary policy, and technological leaps are inextricably linked.
