**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Inflation's Stubborn Grip!**

The **US economy** remains sensitive to inflation, with October's **US Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) price index** showing headline PCE up 0.3% MoM and a scorching 3.8% YoY. Core PCE also accelerated, solidifying expectations for another Fed rate hike in December. This follows last week's hotter-than-expected US CPI report, which sent US Treasury yields surging and the US dollar strengthening. [cite: previous briefings]

Despite inflation concerns, **US Consumer Confidence** surged 11.4 points in November to its highest since January 2025, painting a rosier picture for holiday spending. However, November's **US Flash PMIs** eased slightly to a four-month low, signaling moderating but still robust economic growth. For **US Treasuries, European bonds, and FX**, the hot PCE data and solid consumer confidence strongly reinforce expectations for central bank tightening. The French economy, however, contracted for three consecutive quarters, with a gloomy outlook for 2027. [cite: 17, previous briefings]

**🛢️ Oil & Energy: Hormuz Hustle vs. Surplus Hopes!**

The relentless attacks in the **Strait of Hormuz** continue to inject massive volatility and supply-risk premiums into oil markets. However, despite these tensions, both the International Energy Agency (IEA) and Goldman Sachs earlier in the week continued to forecast a **global oil market surplus** returning in Q4 2026 and extending into 2027. This anticipated surplus, driven by recovering Middle East supply and potentially weaker demand, has helped cap some oil price gains. Energy Aspects also predicts significant global oil stockpiling in 2027, which could tighten the market despite projected surpluses. The IEA still warns that global **diesel supplies will remain "tight" into 2027**. The US pressured Venezuela and Iraq to consider leaving OPEC, aiming to increase their oil production and limit OPEC's influence. [cite: 12, 36, previous briefings]

### Broader Implications: The Volatility Treadmill Accelerates 🎲

The global stage continues its relentless spin on a volatility treadmill, dramatically accelerated by the persistent **Strait of Hormuz crisis**, stubbornly hot **US inflation data**, and President Trump's aggressive new **tariff proposals**.
