### The Week That Was: A Geopolitical Soap Opera & Tech Tidal Wave! 🎭

**🌍 Geopolitics & Conflicts: Summits, Straits, & Strikes!**

President Trump's diplomatic whirlwind culminated in a historic, first-ever **trilateral summit** with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen, China, on November 19. While they issued a "peace declaration" calling for UN reforms, increased global oil supplies, and reduced nuclear risks, it was more symbolic than substantive on the specifics of Ukraine or Iran.

The **Strait of Hormuz** has been nothing short of a raging inferno all week. Beyond the latest incidents, we've seen over 30 incidents in October and at least 22 so far in November, including multiple tankers struck off Oman's Khasab, bringing the total to seven since November 16 alone. The US confirmed Iran is deploying anti-ship cruise missiles, and the UAE is reportedly shuttling oil with US military assistance in a high-stakes operation. The International Maritime Organization (IMO) has tallied 133 commercial vessel incidents and 25 seafarers killed in the region since the conflict began. The maritime security threat level remains SEVERE. Unverified Iranian claims even reported two supertankers hitting mines and one sinking over the weekend. The US also conducted airstrikes on Iran's Qeshm island in retaliation for targeting US assets.

In the relentless **Ukraine conflict**, Ukrainian forces are celebrating an "unprecedented operation" after striking the vital Kerch Bridge on November 16-17 with "Palianytsia drones" and "Neptune missiles," causing significant damage. Russia retaliated with widespread missile and drone attacks on critical energy infrastructure across Ukraine, causing power outages and damaging Kyiv's Justice Ministry headquarters and bridges. Russian drones even violated Moldovan airspace. Earlier US-mediated peace talks in Abu Dhabi (November 10-11) did secure an agreement in principle for a prisoner exchange, but no major breakthrough on ending the war, as European leaders now seek a seat at future discussions. Russia, however, remains firm against NATO membership for Ukraine and European involvement in talks. Separately, allies are warning of a possible Russian nuclear weapon in space.

**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Inflation's Grip, Yield Swings, & Sentiment Shifts!**

The **US economy** remains sensitive to inflation. Last week's hotter-than-expected US CPI report (November 10) solidified market expectations for a December Fed rate hike, sending **US Treasury yields** surging (with the 10-year yield breaking above 5%) and the **US dollar** strengthening significantly. **European bond yields** also climbed, increasing the probability of a December ECB rate hike to around 75%. Meanwhile, the University of Michigan's final consumer sentiment index for November, released on November 20, rose to 51.5 (from 48.0 in October), a 7.3% jump, with year-ahead inflation expectations declining to 3.7% (from 4.7%). US Advance Monthly Retail Sales for October (November 17) showed modest growth, indicating continued consumer demand. Markets are now eagerly awaiting crucial US PCE inflation data on November 25.

**🛢️ Oil & Energy: Hormuz Chaos vs. Supply Surplus Hopes!**

The relentless attacks in the **Strait of Hormuz** continue to inject massive volatility and supply-risk premiums into oil markets. However, despite these tensions, both the International Energy Agency (IEA) and Goldman Sachs issued reports this week forecasting a **global oil market surplus** returning in Q4 2026 and into 2027. This anticipated surplus, driven by recovering Middle East supply and weaker demand, has helped cap some oil price gains. The IEA still warns that global **diesel supplies will remain "tight" into 2027**, although the US Energy Secretary expects prices to fall due to increased refined products and unsanctioned Russian diesel.

**🌾 Agriculture: Triple Threat & Trade Talk!**

Global agricultural markets remain caught in a "triple shock" vortex: **Hormuz instability** snarls crucial fertilizer and energy transport, **Black Sea conflicts** continue to limit vital grain exports (a drone strike tragically sank a wheat cargo off Bulgaria, adding fresh risk premiums), and the looming threat of a **Super El Niño** adds climate uncertainty. The USDA's November WASDE report (November 10) surprised markets by cutting the national corn yield and trimming soybean ending stocks due to robust export demand, though world wheat stocks reportedly grew. China's slow follow-through on its pledged $17 billion annual US farm purchases continues to create demand uncertainty for corn. In other news, Oregon confirmed its first case of bird flu in dairy cattle on November 10.

**💰 Metals: Inflation's Shadow Lingers! ✨**

Precious metals faced a tough week. After October's brutal declines, the "hot" US CPI data delivered another blow, sending **gold** tumbling below $3,900, with **silver** sliding below $46 an ounce and **platinum** below $1,400 earlier in the week, as higher interest rate expectations make non-yielding assets less attractive. However, gold found its footing and staged a rebound of over 1.5% on November 23. The global silver market is still forecast to record its sixth consecutive annual deficit in 2026.

**🤖 Tech & AI Frontier: Nvidia's Shake-Up & Chip Control!**

The **AI industry** was rocked by **Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's resignation** on November 12, amidst an investigation into an alleged affair with a suspected Chinese spy and national security concerns over chip sales to China. While Colette Kress served as interim CEO, President Trump's intervention led to Huang's reinstatement on Monday. Nvidia's board had previously stated it would not reinstate Huang, and had established a permanent committee for national security and export controls on November 17. On the regulatory front, the US Bureau of Industry and Security expanded its scrutiny of overseas data centers and added licensing requirements for remote access to advanced AI chips. OpenAI's Chief Scientist publicly urged the industry to implement voluntary limits on development, emphasizing safety protocols, while Meta's Chief AI Officer declared superintelligence is "arriving." US nonprofits also filed a lawsuit on November 12 against major tech giants demanding a freeze on AI model training due to "catastrophic harm" concerns.
