### The Week That Was: A Geopolitical Soap Opera & Tech Tidal Wave! 🎭

The past week delivered some serious whiplash! Early on, we saw the **US launch "powerful" new strikes against Iran** (July 30) after Iranian missile attacks on US forces in Jordan, quickly followed by joint US-Saudi retaliatory strikes in Iraq. Iran's IRGC also claimed to have *struck and halted three oil tankers* in the Strait of Hormuz. But then, a dramatic pivot! President Trump announced a **tentative "pause" in military strikes** over the weekend, opening the door for diplomacy, with talks slated to start today, August 3rd, to reopen the Strait and address Iran's nuclear ambitions. However, Iran is playing hardball, insisting the **Strait's status won't revert to pre-conflict days**. **💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Inflation Hawks Circle!**
Central banks are truly navigating a minefield! The **Federal Reserve held interest rates steady** (3.5%-3.75%) on July 29th, but here's the kicker: *three policymakers dissented*, pushing for a hike, signaling growing hawkish sentiment fueled by persistent inflation and the Middle East chaos. This hawkish undertone sent the **US 30-year Treasury yield to its highest level since 2007**. Across the pond, the **European Central Bank (ECB)** held rates steady on July 23rd but delivered a "hawkish pause," ready for a potential September hike if surging energy prices fuel inflation. Eurozone growth in Q2 moderated, while German inflation accelerated to a three-month high in July. The **US Dollar's** performance was mixed, experiencing a dip after the FOMC decision. Expect all eyes to be on Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's late August address at Jackson Hole for more monetary policy clues.

**🛢️ Oil & Energy: The Strait's Grip Loosens (Momentarily)!**
Oil prices have been on an absolute heart-stopping ride. After spiking earlier in the week due to renewed US-Iran hostilities and Iran's claims of halting tankers, prices took a **sharp dive on Monday (August 3rd)** following President Trump's announcement of diplomatic talks to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. However, the long-term supply outlook remains tight, with **US commercial crude inventories at their lowest since 2018** and strategic reserves at a 1983 low. Major players like ExxonMobil, Chevron, and Shell are reporting **sky-high profits**, directly benefiting from the conflict-driven surge in crude prices and refining margins. OPEC+ also approved a modest oil production quota increase from September.

The AI race is accelerating at warp speed, with China making huge strides. Adding fuel to the fire, a **Reuters report today alleges Chinese AI firms are using "model distillation"** – essentially learning from and re-training on the outputs of US frontier models by OpenAI and Anthropic – potentially for military applications, igniting concerns about intellectual property theft and national security.

This alarming incident has prompted over 1,000 tech employees, including Anthropic's CEO, to sign a petition urging a US-backed international effort to **"deliberately pace" advanced AI development** due to escalating risks. The massive **AI buildout is also being flagged by Fed Chair Warsh as a new inflation threat**, driving up costs for memory chips, processors, and electricity.
