Germany's 10-year Bund yield hovered around 3.25%, with France's 10-year hitting its highest since 2008 at over 4.13%. German officials openly blamed "Trump's war in Iran" for the surge in bond yields. Markets are still overwhelmingly pricing in an **ECB rate hike in September**. Minutes from the Federal Reserve's July meeting revealed that some officials were prepared to raise rates, keeping the Fed's hawkish posture alive. The **U.S. Dollar** had a volatile week, reacting to economic data and geopolitical shifts, but ended Monday firmer.
