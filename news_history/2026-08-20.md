### The Week That Was: A Geopolitical Soap Opera & Tech Tidal Wave! 🎭

**🌍 Geopolitics & Conflicts: Strait Saga, Red Sea Attacks & Ukrainian Strikes!**
The **Strait of Hormuz** has been a geopolitical pressure cooker all week. After the 60-day ceasefire between the U.S. and Iran expired on Monday (August 17th) without resolution, Iran firmly maintained its demands for concessions before a full reopening. President Trump continued to claim U.S. control, even posting a map claiming the Strait as "New American Territory." While Iran's chief negotiator reiterated the Strait remains closed, the revelation of the *secret U.S. shipping corridor* aims to mitigate oil supply disruptions. Shipping traffic through Hormuz has been severely hampered, though alternative routes and now the U.S. corridor are providing some throughput.

In the **Red Sea**, Iran-backed Houthi forces claimed missile attacks on a Saudi military ship and four escort vessels off Yemen's Mocha on Monday, with initial reports of civilian casualties. Bodies of four sailors killed in an August 11th Houthi missile attack on a Yemeni commercial vessel were repatriated this week. These attacks have caused a reported 40% drop in tanker transits through the strategic Bab el-Mandeb Strait.

The **Black Sea conflict** saw Ukraine launch one of its largest drone attacks on Russia on Sunday, hitting a missile fuel production facility near Moscow. Russia retaliated with strikes on Ukrainian steel plants and Naftogaz facilities. Notably, Russia has been forced to *import fuel from India* due to Ukrainian strikes on its refineries, a significant reversal for a peacetime exporter.

Tragically, the **Colombia earthquake** on August 10th has seen its death toll rise to 314, with extensive damage to homes, schools, and infrastructure across 15 departments. Meanwhile, **Venezuela** continues to grapple with the aftermath of its June earthquakes, with over 6,400 reported dead as of August 2026, and tens of thousands still missing. A magnitude 4.0 aftershock jolted La Guaira on Tuesday, causing alarm but no reported major damage.

**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Yield Rollercoaster & Fed Watch!**
Bond markets were on a rollercoaster! **U.S. Treasury yields** soared earlier in the week, with the 10-year reaching 4.72% and the 30-year hitting a 19-year high above 5.30% on Monday, driven by persistent inflation fears and heavy government borrowing. However, a surprising announcement from the U.S. Treasury on Wednesday about *doubling liquidity support buyback operations* for longer-dated bonds sent yields sharply lower, easing concerns about long-term borrowing costs. This also saw **European government bond yields** retreat from multi-year highs. Money markets are still pricing in over a 90% probability of an ECB rate hike in September. The **U.S. Dollar** had a mixed week, initially softening but then rebounding modestly before drifting lower after the Treasury announcement. Minutes from the Federal Reserve's July meeting released Wednesday indicated that "several" officials were prepared to raise rates, with "many" saying further hikes might be needed if inflation doesn't improve.

**🛢️ Oil & Energy: Hormuz, Inventories & Russian Woes!**
**Oil prices** continued their upward trajectory this week, with Brent crude topping $92 a barrel and WTI hovering around $85, fueled by the prolonged Hormuz standoff and escalating Middle East tensions. The UAE's decision to suspend trade with Iran added further geopolitical risk premium to prices. However, the surprise revelation of the *secret U.S. shipping corridor* in Hormuz could potentially alleviate some supply concerns, though the impact on prices is yet to fully unfold. Meanwhile, Russian oil exports from western ports have fallen due to Black Sea disruptions, and Russia's need to import gasoline due to Ukrainian refinery attacks further highlights global supply constraints.

**🌾 Agriculture: WASDE Aftermath & Crop Tour Insights!**
The **USDA's August WASDE report** on August 12th delivered unexpected cuts to average **U.S. corn yields** (180.7 bpa) and **soybean yields** (52.7 bpa) due to extreme heat and dryness in parts of the Midwest. However, increased planted acreage for both crops boosted overall production, including a record soybean crop. Corn and soybean futures saw significant activity following the report. The ongoing Pro Farmer Crop Tour is finding further yield disappointment, with Indiana corn down 5.3% and Nebraska down 8.8% on last year. **Wheat production forecasts were also cut**, leading to tighter supplies and a raised farm price due to lower stocks and global production headwinds. The persistent **Black Sea conflict** continues to disrupt Ukrainian grain exports, reinforcing market uncertainty.
