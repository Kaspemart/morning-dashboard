### The Week That Was: A Geopolitical Soap Opera & Tech Tidal Wave! 🎭

**🌍 Geopolitics & Conflicts: Hormuz Rollercoaster & Ukraine's Grind!**

The **Strait of Hormuz** has been a dramatic, high-stakes theater. Earlier in the week, it was a full-blown war zone, with Brent crude surging past $100 a barrel. Reports of US forces destroying Iranian crude oil tankers and Iran's Revolutionary Guards claiming ballistic missile strikes on US destroyers and a US base in Jordan painted a grim picture. Shipping traffic remained severely suppressed, with only a dozen commodity vessels transiting over the weekend, far below usual levels. Iran's Parliament Speaker had declared Tehran wouldn't reopen the Strait until its conditions, including recognizing Iran's rights, fulfilling US commitments, ending hostilities, releasing frozen funds, and lifting the naval blockade, were met. The US also warned its citizens to leave Iran immediately, citing a complex security environment. Beyond Hormuz, Houthi rebels continued missile and drone attacks on Saudi military targets, forcing Saudi Arabia to close its crucial East-West pipeline.

In the brutal **Ukraine conflict**, Russia intensified its drone and missile attacks on Ukrainian cities and infrastructure, including advanced, faster jet-powered drones. Ukraine, in turn, escalated with long-range drone strikes on Russian oil infrastructure, including a significant 3,000 km attack on an Arctic gas facility in Yamalo-Nenets, and later, the Moscow Oil Refinery was set ablaze. Ukrainian President Zelenskyy is pushing for "new long-range operations" and a ceasefire on energy targets amidst US President Trump's push for de-escalation, but the Kremlin remains committed to its maximalist war aims and will not halt military operations during negotiations. A bombshell development from Germany saw prosecutors formally accuse **Ukrainian "state authorities" of ordering the 2022 Nord Stream pipeline sabotage**.

**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Yields Rocket & DLT Dawn!**

Central banks were on a tightening spree! The **Federal Reserve hiked its key interest rate by 25 basis points to a target range of 3.75% to 4.00% on September 16th**, the first increase since July 2023. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh signaled more hikes could be on the horizon, emphasizing the Fed's commitment to price stability, with markets now pricing in another 25 basis-point Fed increase by year-end. This hawkish stance, combined with robust US economic data, sent **US Treasury yields soaring**, with the 10-year briefly hitting 5% before easing slightly.

Across the pond, the **European Central Bank (ECB) hiked its key interest rate by 25 basis points to 2.50% on September 10th**, effective September 16th, to combat surging Eurozone inflation, which hit 3.3% in August. The ECB also launched "Pontes" on September 21st, a new initiative to provide wholesale settlement for tokenized financial assets, linking distributed ledger technology (DLT) based markets to TARGET Services. The ECB is even starting preparatory work to invest a small portion of its own funds in tokenized euro-denominated securities to gain experience with DLT. European government bond yields similarly reached multi-year highs.

**🛢️ Oil & Energy: Hormuz Hustle & Supply Squeeze!**

**Oil prices** have been a rollercoaster, surging past $100 a barrel and hitting $107.63 due to the escalating chaos in the Strait of Hormuz and Red Sea. The International Energy Agency (IEA) slashed its 2026 world oil supply projection by 1.3 million barrels per day. Saudi Arabia's ability to export oil was under severe strain, with major maritime routes and a key crude pipeline affected, though the East-West Pipeline has reportedly restarted operations. Russia's oil industry continues to scramble for gasoline imports due to ongoing Ukrainian drone attacks on its refineries. US diesel prices have surged above $6 per gallon, with some California stations hitting nearly $10, and high diesel prices are expected to continue for years.

**🌾 Agriculture: WASDE Updates & Triple Shock!**

The **USDA's September WASDE report**, released on September 11th, brought notable shifts. The US corn yield forecast for 2026/27 was lowered, leading to a cut in production, and the season-average farm price for corn was raised. In a surprise move, the US soybean yield forecast was *increased* to near record highs, partly due to increased Chinese purchases, and the season-average soybean price forecast jumped significantly. Global wheat production was also raised due to gains in Australia, Canada, Ukraine, and Argentina. Global agricultural commodity prices have risen noticeably since September due to a "triple shock": Hormuz instability (affecting fertilizer and energy transport), Black Sea conflicts (constraining grain exports), and expectations of a Super El Niño. India also cut duties on crude palm, soy, and sunflower oils, effective today.
