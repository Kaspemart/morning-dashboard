### The Week That Was: A Geopolitical Soap Opera & Tech Tidal Wave! 🎭

**🌍 Geopolitics & Conflicts: Hormuz, Ukraine, & a Blue Tsunami!**

The **Strait of Hormuz** has been a geopolitical pressure cooker all week! Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) claimed several oil tankers exploded after hitting naval mines, even boasting they disabled a St Kitts & Nevis-flagged bulker trying to bypass the US naval blockade. Adding to the drama, Iran declared that only vessels from India, China, Iraq, and Qatar would get free passage, a clear jab at US allies. The UKMTO reports shipping traffic through the Strait is still "well below normal."

In the relentless **Ukraine conflict**, Russia continued its barrage of missile and drone attacks on critical infrastructure, causing widespread power outages. Ukrainian forces, however, kept up their counter-strikes on Russian oil refineries and logistics centers. Before today's talks, President Trump had even hinted that Ukraine's territory might see a "different shape" in any future agreement, raising eyebrows.

Stateside, the **US Midterm Elections** on November 3 delivered a stunning "blue tsunami," giving Democrats control of both the House and Senate. This is widely seen as a "crushing rebuke" to President Trump, particularly concerning his economic policies and the ongoing Iran war, and could prompt a rethink on tariff policies.

**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Inflation's Grip & Yield Swings!**

**US Treasury yields** have been on a wild ride, initially seeing some dips but then bouncing back with upward pressure, especially after stronger-than-expected US employment data from Friday. Markets are now leaning more towards another Fed rate hike in December. The US dollar, after an initial post-midterm wobble, showed resilience. The Treasury also has a refunding operation underway this week with auctions for 3-, 10-, and 30-year notes/bonds.

Across the pond, the Euro is still under pressure from sluggish economic growth. European rate watchers are betting on one more ECB hike in December. French and peripheral bond spreads are anticipated to remain under pressure due to ongoing fiscal concerns. The Bank of England, however, didn't hesitate, hiking its interest rate by 25 basis points to 4% last week.

**🛢️ Oil & Energy: Hormuz Hustle & Diesel Drought!**

The escalating attacks in the **Strait of Hormuz** kept global oil markets on tenterhooks, constantly fueling fears of wider Middle East conflict and disrupting supply. While oil prices plunged today on Trump's "war over" comments, the underlying tensions remain. The International Energy Agency (IEA) continues its dire warnings that global **diesel supplies will remain "tight" into 2027** due to persistent conflict disruptions and refinery damage. On a brighter note for the planet, global CO2 emissions are projected to decrease by a small margin in 2026, driven by the expansion of solar and wind power, and notably, the impact of the Iran conflict.

**🌾 Agriculture: Triple Shock Continues!**

Global agricultural commodity markets are stuck on a "triple shock" treadmill: **Hormuz instability** is snarling crucial fertilizer and energy transport, **Black Sea conflicts** continue to limit vital grain exports, and the looming threat of a **Super El Niño** adds another layer of climate uncertainty. The Hormuz blockade has specifically triggered a severe global fertilizer supply crunch, pushing up prices for inputs. In the Philippines, farmers protested rising production costs (fertilizer, fuel) and low farmgate prices, exacerbated by imports. However, the US Department of Agriculture launched a trade mission to Vietnam to expand export opportunities for American soybeans, corn, and distillers dried grains.
