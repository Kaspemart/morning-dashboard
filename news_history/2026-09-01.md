**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Jackson Hole Jitters & Hawkish Vibes!**

Bond markets were on a wild ride, heavily influenced by Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh's keynote address at the Jackson Hole symposium. Warsh's speech on Friday indicated that inflation in the U.S. is "still too high" and hinted that the central bank "may have to act," suggesting potential further rate hikes. He stressed the Fed's 2% price stability objective is a "firm fixed target" and that recent inflation data don't show "meaningful improvement." This hawkish tone sent U.S. long-dated Treasury yields stubbornly high and strengthened the U.S. dollar, impacting gold prices. Economists now forecast three 0.25 percentage point increases in the Federal Funds rate in September, December, and March.

**Eurozone government bond yields** remained near 15-year highs, with German inflation edging higher to 2.9% in August, adding to the case for an **ECB rate hike in September**. Markets are still heavily pricing in an ECB rate hike next month, pushing the deposit facility rate to 2.50%. German officials have openly blamed "Trump's war in Iran" for the surge in bond yields.
