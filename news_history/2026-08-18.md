**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Inflation Hawks & Yield Volatility!**
Central banks are walking a very narrow tightrope! The **Federal Reserve held interest rates steady** at 3.5%-3.75% on July 29th, but three policymakers dissented, pushing for a hike, signaling persistent hawkish sentiment due to inflation and Middle East instability. Last week's unexpectedly **weak U.S. jobs report for July** (a loss of 23,000 jobs) initially shifted market expectations toward a potential Fed pause in September. However, mixed inflation data has complicated the outlook. The **July CPI report (August 12th) came in at a modest 3.4% year-over-year**, in line with expectations, but the **July Producer Price Index (PPI) on August 13th, while flat monthly, showed core PPI up 0.2% and annual PPI at 4.7%**, leading some to reconsider September rate hike odds. Money markets are now pricing in a **more than 90% probability of an ECB rate hike in September**.
**U.S. Treasury yields saw significant volatility**, with the 10-year yield climbing to 4.72% and the 30-year yield hitting a 19-year high above 5.30% on Monday, reflecting both inflation concerns and heavy government borrowing. **Eurozone government bond yields remained near 15-year highs** on Monday, with Germany's 10-year Bund yield holding around 3.20%, due to concerns that the prolonged Iran war could keep energy prices and inflation elevated. The U.S. Treasury also sanctioned crypto exchanges allegedly funding Iran's IRGC. The **U.S. Dollar** had a mixed week, softening after soft U.S. data but recovering slightly.

The agricultural landscape was significantly impacted by the **USDA's August WASDE report on August 12th.** The report delivered unexpected cuts to average **U.S. corn yields** (180.7 bpa) and **soybean yields** (52.7 bpa) due to extreme heat and dryness in parts of the Midwest. However, increased planted acreage for both crops boosted overall production, including a record soybean crop. The persistent **Black Sea conflict continues to severely impact Ukrainian grain exports**, with recent attacks on Russian grain terminals reinforcing uncertainty around regional grain flows.
