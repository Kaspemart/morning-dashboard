**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Rate Riddles and Yield Swings!**
Central banks are walking a tightrope! The **Federal Reserve held rates steady** yesterday (July 29), a decision that saw three policymakers dissent in favor of a hike, indicating a strong debate within the committee. The Fed's statement acknowledged that "inflation remains elevated" partly due to "supply shocks that have driven price increases in certain sectors, including energy." This triggered a **surge in US 10-year and 30-year Treasury yields**, with the 30-year hitting a 19-year high, as the market digested the hawkish tone on inflation from the central bank and the lack of clear forward guidance. Across the pond, the **European Central Bank (ECB)** last week held rates steady but issued a "hawkish pause," ready for a potential September hike if surging energy prices ignite further inflation. Eurozone government bond yields climbed in reaction to the US Treasury sell-off and rising oil prices. The US Dollar generally acted as a safe haven during turmoil.

Canadian oat production is also seeing dramatic cuts, leading to tight ending stocks. On the tech front, John Deere's acquisition of drone technology company Sentera aims to transform crop management, and 360 Yield's autonomous irrigation system, 360 Rain, is gaining traction.
