**🛢️ Oil & Energy: Hormuz Hustle, Strategic Reserves, & Diesel Tightness!**

**Oil prices** were a dizzying rollercoaster this week, initially surging past $108 a barrel due to escalating Hormuz tensions and tanker attacks, before dipping on President Trump's "energy ceasefire" claims. **🌾 Agriculture: Harvest Headaches & USDA Shocks!**

Global agricultural commodity prices continue to face a "triple shock": Hormuz instability disrupting crucial fertilizer and energy transport, Black Sea conflicts limiting vital grain exports, and the ongoing threat of a Super El Niño. The Hormuz blockade has sparked a severe global fertilizer supply crunch, pushing up diammonium phosphate prices, which in turn has driven corn and soybean prices higher. A UN agency warned this week that Hormuz shipping disruption is already hitting harvests and threatening food security. Farmers are reportedly reducing fertilizer applications, which could impact future global yields.

The **USDA's October WASDE report** on October 9 delivered some surprises, shockingly raising the national average corn yield and boosting production, initially sending corn futures lower. Soybean yield was also raised slightly, leading to record production. However, corn and wheat futures moved lower later in the week due to harvest pressure, even with delayed US fall harvests due to historic September rainfall. Brazil's soybean planting is proceeding at its fastest pace ever, while Northern Hemisphere winter wheat planting is behind pace and into dry soils. Separately, a Michigan cyclospora outbreak sickened 14,000 people and caused millions in agricultural losses.
