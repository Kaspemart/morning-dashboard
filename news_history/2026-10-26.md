### The Week That Was: A Geopolitical Soap Opera & Tech Tidal Wave! 🎭

**🌍 Geopolitics & Conflicts: Hormuz Ablaze, Ukraine's Unending Fight, & European Unrest!**

The **Strait of Hormuz** remains an absolute inferno! Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) Navy claimed a staggering *seven* oil tankers either caught fire or were damaged overnight on October 22 after being hit or striking naval mines. This unprecedented surge in attacks further elevates the already "severe" shipping threat, with October seeing the most reported incidents (28) since the start of the Iran War in February. Iran's IRGC Navy continues to warn that vessels using "unauthorized routes" would be held responsible, while the US State Department has even ordered non-essential personnel to leave Saudi Arabia following attacks on regional oil fields and airports, fueling fears of a wider conflict. Iran continues to reject US proposals for reopening the waterway, deeming them "insufficient." Houthi rebels are also maintaining their drone and missile attacks on Saudi airports, including deadly strikes on Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport.

In the **Ukraine conflict**, there's a delicate and dangerous diplomatic dance. Russia pounded Kyiv with ballistic missiles and drones on October 22, killing at least two and injuring 22, just hours after Russian President Putin reportedly agreed "in principle" to resume three-way peace talks with the US and Ukraine following the US midterm elections. Ukrainian President Zelenskyy, while backing the talks, remains cautious and has publicly denied President Trump's earlier claims of an "energy ceasefire" and a deal for the US to buy Russian diesel. Ukraine also continued its strikes on Russian oil refineries, hitting a facility in the Ryazan region. Russia, in turn, claims to have intercepted over 1,000 Ukrainian drones. Beyond these major conflicts, **France** remains gripped by nationwide protests over rising living costs and poor school conditions, leading to widespread rioting and looting. Italy also plans troop deployment amidst widening protests. In Canada, a tragic explosion and fire at **CFB Wainwright**, a military base, resulted in four confirmed deaths and dozens of injuries during a training exercise.

**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Inflation's Jolt & Yield Uncertainty!**

**US Treasury yields** saw a volatile week, with the 10-year yield falling below 5% for the first time in a month due to a flight to safety. This flight was fueled by growing concerns over France's gaping budget deficit and the broader impact of rising oil prices on economic growth. However, a softer-than-expected demand was noted in the US Treasury auction of five-year Inflation Protected Securities (TIPS). **European government bond yields** remained under pressure, with French 10-year yields hitting 5% for the first time since 2002, and spreads over German Bunds widening significantly due to fiscal concerns and political instability. This volatility in sovereign bond markets is no longer just a rates story; it's driving changes in risk appetite and corporate credit spreads.

In **FX markets**, the US dollar saw mixed movements, while the euro remained near $1.12, influenced by shifting Treasury yields and France's debt worries. Meanwhile, persistent inflation concerns, evidenced by the UK's September CPI at 3.5%, and a resilient US labor market (lower jobless claims) are keeping expectations for central bank rate hikes high, with a December Fed hike still a strong probability despite cautious signals from some Fed officials.

**🛢️ Oil & Energy: Hormuz Hustle, Geopolitical Premiums, & Diesel Drought!**

**Oil prices** were a dizzying rollercoaster, surging earlier in the week with Brent crude topping $112 and WTI above $100 a barrel. This was largely driven by the escalating Hormuz attacks, drone strikes on a Saudi Aramco oilfield, and fears of potential US and Israeli strikes on Iran post-midterms. Despite recent reports of oil flows out of the Persian Gulf returning to pre-war levels, the cost of shipping crude remains elevated due to continued attacks and thin US commercial crude inventories, which fell for a third consecutive week. The International Energy Agency (IEA) continues to warn that global **diesel supplies will remain "tight" into 2027** due to conflict disruptions.

**🌾 Agriculture: Soybeans Soar, Grains Grapple!**

Chicago grain futures had a mixed week. **Soybeans** saw a strong rally, breaking an eight-session losing streak, thanks to renewed Chinese buying and robust US export sales. This rebound was particularly notable as it occurred during the US harvest, typically a time of price pressure. In contrast, **corn and wheat futures** moved lower, largely due to seasonal harvest pressure in the US and an easing of geopolitical risk premiums, despite ongoing uncertainties around Black Sea grain shipments. Brazilian corn shipments to Europe are reportedly increasing, while Argentina's 2026/27 wheat production forecast has been raised.

**💰 Metals: Gold's Dip, Silver's Slide! ✨**

Precious metals faced a challenging week. **Gold** plunged over 1% on October 22, falling to its lowest since mid-October, around $4030-$4068 per ounce. **Silver** saw an even stronger sell-off, dropping nearly 5%, and **platinum** also declined. This widespread retreat in precious metals is attributed to renewed inflation worries, rising Treasury yields, and a stronger US dollar, all of which make non-yielding assets less attractive.
