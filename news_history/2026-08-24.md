**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Yields on a Rollercoaster!**
Bond markets were on a wild ride! **U.S. Treasury yields** soared earlier in the week, with the 10-year hitting 4.72% and the 30-year reaching a 19-year high above 5.30%, driven by persistent inflation fears and heavy government borrowing. However, the **U.S. Treasury's surprise announcement on Wednesday** to double its liquidity support buyback operations for longer-dated bonds to at least $4 billion per operation provided a brief reprieve, sending yields tumbling and causing the U.S. dollar to weaken while gold surged. This intervention signals an increasingly "interventionist approach" to containing borrowing costs. The relief was short-lived, with yields climbing again by Friday, as underlying debt worries persisted.
**Eurozone government bond yields** remained near 15-year highs, with Germany's 10-year Bund yield at 3.25% and France's at 4.10%. Markets are still pricing in over a 90% probability of an **ECB rate hike in September**. Minutes from the Federal Reserve's July meeting released Wednesday indicated that "several" officials were prepared to raise rates, with "many" saying further hikes might be needed if inflation doesn't improve.

However, increased planted acreage for both crops boosted overall production, including a record soybean crop.
