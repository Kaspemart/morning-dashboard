### The Week That Was: A Geopolitical Soap Opera & Tech Tidal Wave! 🎭

**🌍 Geopolitics & Conflicts: Strait Saga Deepens & Global Hotspots Flare!**
The past week was a whirlwind, dominated by the **Strait of Hormuz deadlock**. The 60-day ceasefire between the U.S. and Iran expired on Monday (August 17th) with no resolution in sight. Iran remained steadfast, insisting the Strait is "Iran's today, and will remain Iranian territory," and that it won't fully reopen until the U.S. meets its demands for compensation, lifted sanctions, and an end to military threats. President Trump, meanwhile, continued to claim U.S. control and even threatened to bomb Oman if it interfered with U.S. efforts, despite Oman trying to broker a separate shipping deal. Shipping traffic remains severely hampered, though alternative routes are gaining traction. The Pentagon is now even evaluating a smaller U.S. military presence in the Persian Gulf after Iranian strikes on U.S. bases.

Beyond Hormuz, **Iran-backed Houthi forces in Yemen** continued their Red Sea attacks, claiming missile strikes on a Saudi military ship and escorts, which reportedly killed civilians and caused a 40% drop in tanker transits through the strategic Bab el-Mandeb Strait. In the ongoing **Black Sea conflict**, Ukraine launched one of its largest drone attacks on Russia on Sunday, hitting a missile fuel production facility near Moscow. Russia retaliated with strikes on Ukrainian steel plants and Naftogaz facilities. Russia also issued threats to the UK over alleged use of British-made drones. Meanwhile, **North Korea** fired a ballistic missile ahead of joint U.S.-South Korea military exercises, which President Trump then scaled back.

**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Inflation Hawks & Yield Volatility!**
Central banks are walking a tightrope! The **Federal Reserve** held interest rates steady on July 29th, but dissenters pushed for a hike, reflecting persistent hawkish sentiment. Last week's weak U.S. jobs report initially cooled rate hike bets, but mixed inflation data (modest CPI, hotter core PPI) this week complicated the outlook. As a result, **U.S. Treasury yields** saw significant volatility, with the 10-year climbing to 4.75% and the 30-year hitting a 19-year high above 5.30%, driven by inflation concerns and heavy government borrowing. **Eurozone government bond yields** also remained near 15-year highs, with markets now pricing in over a 90% probability of an ECB rate hike in September. The **U.S. Dollar** had a mixed week, influenced by economic data and geopolitical tensions.

**🛢️ Oil & Energy: Hormuz Leverage & Supply Squeeze!**
**Oil prices** have been on a wild rollercoaster, directly tied to the Middle East's instability. After dipping on earlier diplomatic hopes, prices **climbed sharply** this week, with Brent pushing past $91, as Iran's hardened stance on the Strait of Hormuz signaled prolonged disruption. The Strait, normally a transit point for a fifth of global oil and LNG, remains a critical choke point. U.S. commercial crude inventories are at their lowest since 2018, keeping the long-term supply outlook tight.

**🌾 Agriculture: WASDE Shakes Things Up & Black Sea Woes!**
The **USDA's August WASDE report** on August 12th delivered unexpected cuts to average **U.S. corn yields** (180.7 bpa) and **soybean yields** (52.7 bpa) due to extreme heat and dryness, though increased planted acreage boosted overall production to a record soybean crop. Corn and soybean futures saw significant activity. **Wheat production forecasts were also cut**, leading to tighter supplies. The persistent **Black Sea conflict** continues to impact Ukrainian grain exports, with recent attacks on Russian grain terminals reinforcing market uncertainty. China remains an active buyer of U.S. soybeans.

**💰 Metals: Safe Haven Swings & Industrial Demand! ✨**
Precious metals have been direct barometers of geopolitical tensions and central bank signals. **Gold and silver surged** last week on weaker U.S. jobs data and mild CPI, but then **retreated slightly** due to mixed PPI data and profit-taking. They remain supported by geopolitical safe-haven demand and strong central bank buying. **Silver faces a sixth straight annual supply deficit in 2026**. **Platinum prices pushed above $1,760**, driven by supply concerns. **Copper prices on the LME rose** due to supply worries and robust demand from the booming AI infrastructure.
