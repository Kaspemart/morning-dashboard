**🌾 Agriculture: Soybeans Soar, Grains Grapple!**

The Hormuz blockade has particularly sparked a severe global fertilizer supply crunch, pushing up diammonium phosphate prices, and in turn, corn and soybean prices. The USDA's October WASDE report surprisingly raised national average corn and soybean yields. **Soybeans** saw a strong rally this week, breaking an eight-session losing streak, thanks to renewed Chinese buying and robust US export sales, even during the US harvest season. In contrast, **corn and wheat futures** moved lower, largely due to seasonal harvest pressure in the US and an easing of geopolitical risk premiums, despite ongoing Black Sea uncertainties. President Trump announced an $8 billion "Harvest Fuel Relief" program to provide one-time payments to farmers to offset high input costs. ### Broader Implications: The Volatility Treadmill Accelerates 🎲

The global stage continues its relentless spin on a volatility treadmill. The raging **Strait of Hormuz crisis** remains the primary accelerant for global energy prices and, by extension, inflation, directly impacting central bank decisions.
