Central banks are walking a very narrow tightrope! The **Federal Reserve held interest rates steady** at 3.5%-3.75% on July 29th, but three policymakers dissented, pushing for a hike, signaling persistent hawkish sentiment. Last week's unexpectedly **weak U.S. jobs report for July (a loss of 23,000 jobs)** had initially shifted market expectations toward a potential Fed pause in September. However, the Fed's August inflation forecast projecting a hotter core PCE for July (up nearly 0.3%) and the mixed CPI/PPI data this week have complicated the outlook. The **July CPI report on August 12th came in at a modest 3.4% year-over-year**, in line with expectations. However, the **July Producer Price Index (PPI) on August 13th, while flat monthly, showed core PPI up 0.2% and annual PPI at 4.7%**, leading some to reconsider September rate hike odds.
**U.S. Treasury yields saw volatility**, with the 10-year yield easing to around 4.67% on August 13th after hitting 4.72% earlier in the week. This reflects both inflation concerns and a rising term premium due to growing U.S. budget deficits. **European bond yields** generally declined due to the mild U.S. inflation data and some easing in oil prices. The U.S. Treasury sanctioned crypto exchanges allegedly funding Iran's IRGC.

The agricultural landscape was significantly impacted by the **USDA's August WASDE report on August 12th.** The report delivered unexpected cuts to average **U.S. corn yields** (180.7 bpa) and **soybean yields** (52.7 bpa) due to extreme heat and dryness. However, increased planted acreage for both crops boosted overall production, including a record soybean crop. Corn and soybean futures saw significant activity in response. **Wheat production forecasts were also cut**, leading to tighter supplies and a raised farm price due to lower stocks and global production headwinds. **China remains an active buyer of U.S. soybeans.** The persistent **Black Sea conflict continues to severely impact Ukrainian grain exports**, with recent attacks on Russian grain terminals reinforcing uncertainty around regional grain flows.
