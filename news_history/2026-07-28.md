### The Week That Was: A Geopolitical Soap Opera & Tech Tidal Wave! 🎭

**🌍 Geopolitics & Conflicts: From Inferno to Tentative Truce!**
The past week saw the US-Iran conflict reach a fever pitch, with the US launching its 12th and 13th consecutive nights of airstrikes against Iran, targeting IRGC naval bases, missile sites, and critical infrastructure. President Trump threatened to bomb Iranian bridges or power plants for any attacks on shipping, and the IRGC dramatically **seized a Liberian-flagged oil tanker** near the Strait of Hormuz, accusing it of "violating maritime law." The Strait of Hormuz remained largely shut for commercial traffic, with shipping down 90-95% from pre-conflict levels. Adding to the maritime mayhem, Iran-backed **Houthi forces in Yemen attacked Saudi oil tankers in the Red Sea** and announced a naval blockade, forcing vessels to make U-turns. The UN and IEA expressed serious concerns about global energy security. However, the latest news shows a significant de-escalation, with both the US and Iran pausing strikes over the weekend, and diplomatic talks through Oman making progress.

Elsewhere, the **Venezuela earthquake disaster** continued its tragic toll, with fatalities officially at 4,333. Ukrainian drone attacks on Russian vessels in the **Sea of Azov and Black Sea** intensified, leading to a full suspension of shipping activity in the Sea of Azov and disrupting Ukraine's Black Sea shipping corridor. Russia's Defense Ministry claimed it was attacking ships and ports supporting Ukraine's war efforts, with Ukraine asserting Russia has struck 28 commercial ships in the past month.

**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Inflation Juggling & Hawkish Pauses!**
Central banks are walking a tightrope! The **Federal Reserve** will announce its July interest rate decision this week, with markets expecting rates to be held steady, but keeping a keen eye on signals for future hikes due to renewed inflation risks from Middle East tensions and AI-driven demand. Earlier cooler June inflation data (CPI eased to 3.5%, PPI fell 0.3%) had pared back July hike bets, but a September hike remains a strong possibility. The **European Central Bank (ECB)** held its interest rates steady last week, as expected, but President Lagarde issued a "hawkish pause," warning that the full inflationary impact of the energy shock is yet to play out and keeping a September hike on the table. US 10-year Treasury yields, which had hovered around two-month highs of 4.63%, are likely to react to the shifting geopolitical landscape and Fed signals. The US Dollar, acting as a safe haven during the peak of Mideast turmoil, will also be closely watched for movements.

**🛢️ Oil & Energy: The Strait's Grip Loosens (for now)!**
Oil prices have been on a wild ride, directly mirroring the Strait of Hormuz chaos. After surging to two-month highs, with Brent briefly topping $100 a barrel and WTI crossing $90 due to the US naval blockade, Iranian threats, and tanker seizures, the recent de-escalation brought prices tumbling down 4-5% on Monday. However, shipping through the critical Strait of Hormuz and the Bab el-Mandeb Strait remains significantly hampered, and analysts like Goldman Sachs still warn Brent could hit $120 if disruptions persist.

**🌾 Agriculture: War, Weather, and Chinese Demand!**
The **USDA's July WASDE report** (July 10) brought mixed news: new-crop US corn ending stocks were cut due to increased export demand, soybean ending stocks held steady (with a projected record US production), and US wheat ending stocks were lowered, pointing to the smallest US wheat production since 1970. Hot weather in the US Midwest remains a concern for corn and soybeans. Meanwhile, the escalating **Black Sea conflict** has severely impacted Ukrainian grain exports, with no traffic reported on its shipping corridor for the first time since 2023, keeping wheat prices elevated despite some profit-taking. China's appetite for US soybeans continued, with new flash sales reported. Concerns are also growing over the decline in public investment in US agricultural research, contrasting with China's booming investment.

**💰 Metals: Safe Haven Swings! ✨**
Precious metals have been a direct barometer of geopolitical tensions and inflation expectations. **Gold, silver, platinum, and palladium all saw gains on Monday** as the pause in US-Iran fighting eased oil supply risks and inflation concerns. This follows a period of volatility where gold and silver had been caught between rising oil prices (geopolitical risk) and the Fed's ongoing hawkish tone. Central banks are reportedly still keen buyers on price dips. China maintains its tight grip on rare earth export controls, while **Guardian Metal's Pilot Mountain Tungsten Project** in Nevada (backed by US Defense Production Act investment) released positive Pre-Feasibility Study results, signaling a potential new US tungsten source.
