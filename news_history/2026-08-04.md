**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Inflation Hawks Circle & Yields Rollercoaster!**
Central banks are truly navigating a minefield! The **Federal Reserve held interest rates steady** (3.5%-3.75%) on July 29th, but *three policymakers dissented*, pushing for a hike, signaling growing hawkish sentiment fueled by persistent inflation and the Middle East chaos. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh reiterated the Fed's unwavering commitment to its 2% inflation target and indicated his upcoming Jackson Hole speech in August would focus on "big questions" rather than short-term market reactions, emphasizing the Fed is "not constrained by market prices". This hawkish undertone sent the **US 30-year Treasury yield to its highest level since 2007** last week, and the 10-year yield finished near 4.74%. Across the pond, the **European Central Bank (ECB)** held rates steady on July 23rd but delivered a "hawkish pause," ready for a potential September hike if surging energy prices fuel inflation. Eurozone growth in Q2 moderated, while German inflation accelerated to a three-month high in July. The **US Dollar's** performance was mixed, experiencing a dip after the FOMC decision.

Oil prices have been on an absolute heart-stopping ride. The UK's Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) is now actively monitoring the situation, marking one of the first formal regulatory responses. The massive **AI buildout is also being flagged by Fed Chair Warsh as a new inflation threat**, driving up costs for memory chips, processors, and electricity.
