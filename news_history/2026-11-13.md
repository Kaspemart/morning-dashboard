### The Week That Was: A Geopolitical Soap Opera & Tech Tidal Wave! 🎭

**🌍 Geopolitics & Conflicts: Hormuz Ablaze, Ukraine's Unending Fight, & a Blue Tsunami!**

The **Strait of Hormuz** has been nothing short of a raging inferno all week. Beyond yesterday's incidents, the International Maritime Organization (IMO) reported 133 confirmed commercial vessel incidents and 25 seafarers killed in the region since the conflict began. Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) has repeatedly claimed full control of the Strait, issuing warnings to vessels using "unauthorized routes" and even stating that only ships from India, China, Iraq, and Qatar would be allowed free passage. This volatile situation kept oil markets on edge. President Trump did declare the war on Iran "effectively over" on Tuesday, November 10, sending oil prices dipping, but fresh attacks quickly reignited supply concerns.

In the relentless **Ukraine conflict**, Russia continued its devastating missile and drone barrages on critical energy infrastructure, causing widespread power outages. Ukrainian forces, however, kept up their counter-strikes on Russian oil refineries and logistics centers, hitting five significant refineries and smaller ones between October 30 and November 11. Russian President Putin has reiterated his refusal to compromise on territorial demands, including full control over Luhansk, Donetsk, Zaporizhia, and Kherson oblasts.

Stateside, the **US Midterm Elections** on November 3 delivered a stunning "blue tsunami," with Democrats seizing control of both the House and Senate. This is widely seen as a "crushing rebuke" to President Trump's administration, especially concerning his economic policies and the prolonged Iran war, potentially leading to a rethinking of tariff strategies.

**💰 Market Movers & Central Bank Whispers: Inflation's Grip & Yield Swings!**

The **US Consumer Price Index (CPI) for October**, released on Tuesday, November 10, came in hotter than expected, with headline CPI up 0.4% month-over-month and core inflation increasing 0.3%. This immediately sent **US Treasury yields** surging, with the 10-year hitting 4.99% and the 2-year seeing its largest daily increase since April 2025. The **US dollar** strengthened significantly as markets now fully price in a December Fed rate hike, with an 11% chance of a 50-basis-point increase. **European bond yields** also climbed, and the probability of a December ECB rate hike jumped to around 75%. Both the Federal Reserve and the European Central Bank (ECB) had held interest rates steady last week, but the fresh inflation data has drastically shifted expectations. The Bank of England, however, bucked the trend by hiking its interest rate by 25 basis points to 4% last week.

**🛢️ Oil & Energy: Hormuz Hustle & Diesel Drought!**

Beyond the dramatic price swings driven by Hormuz incidents and Trump's comments, the International Energy Agency (IEA) continues to warn that global **diesel supplies will remain "tight" into 2027** due to persistent conflict disruptions and refinery damage. US commercial crude inventories saw an unexpected increase of 4.7 million barrels in the week ended November 6, while gasoline inventories also rose. Meanwhile, a new report at COP31 declared the world is firmly in the "age of electricity," with more than double the electricity generation expected in the next decade.

**🌾 Agriculture: Triple Shock Continues, WASDE Surprises, & Bird Flu Scare!**

Global agricultural markets remain on a "triple shock" treadmill: **Hormuz instability** snarls crucial fertilizer and energy transport, **Black Sea conflicts** limit vital grain exports, and the looming threat of a **Super El Niño** adds climate uncertainty. The Hormuz blockade has particularly triggered a severe global fertilizer supply crunch. The USDA's November WASDE report (November 10) provided surprises, cutting the national corn yield and trimming soybean ending stocks due to robust export demand, though world wheat stocks reportedly grew. On the livestock front, Oregon confirmed its first case of highly pathogenic avian influenza (bird flu) in dairy cattle on November 10, putting a commercial dairy farm under quarantine. This follows a farmworker death in Washington from the virus.

**💰 Metals: October's Pain Lingers! ✨**

October was brutal for precious metals, with silver plunging over 19%, platinum down almost 17%, and gold dropping over 7%, hitting a one-year low. This week, the "hot" US CPI data delivered another blow, sending **gold** tumbling nearly 3% yesterday, November 12, to below $3,900, with **silver** sliding below $50 an ounce and **platinum** also seeing significant declines. Higher interest rate expectations make non-yielding assets less attractive. The global silver market is forecast to record its sixth consecutive annual deficit in 2026.
